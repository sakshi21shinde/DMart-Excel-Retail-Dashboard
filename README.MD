# EX10: Designing an Interactive Sales Dashboard for Management

## 📊 D-Mart Retail Business Dashboard

### 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on designing an **interactive D-Mart Retail Business Dashboard** using Excel/WPS Spreadsheet.

The dashboard provides a visual summary of retail sales performance and helps management analyze **sales, revenue, profit, regions, categories, cities, and quantity sold** through interactive filters and charts.

The dashboard is designed to present important business information in a simple and management-friendly format.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Design an interactive retail sales dashboard.
- Analyze total sales and net revenue.
- Compare sales performance across regions.
- Analyze category-wise sales.
- Track monthly profit trends.
- Analyze city-wise sales performance.
- Identify the top-performing cities.
- Compare quantity sold across product categories.
- Provide interactive filtering using slicers.
- Present business insights through charts and KPIs.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Excel / WPS Spreadsheet
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Slicers
- Excel Formulas
- Conditional Formatting
- Charts
- Data Analysis
- Dashboard Design

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dashboard is created using a **D-Mart retail sales dataset**.

The dataset contains retail sales information that can be analyzed based on:

- Region
- Category
- City
- Sales
- Revenue
- Profit
- Quantity

---

# 📈 Dashboard Features

## 1. KPI Cards

The top section of the dashboard contains important Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

### Total Sales
Displays the overall sales generated from the dataset.

### Net Revenue
Displays the calculated net revenue generated from the business.

### Regions
Shows the number of regions included in the analysis.

### Category
Shows the number of product categories included in the dataset.

These KPI cards provide management with a quick overview of business performance.

---

## 2. Region Slicer

An interactive **Region Slicer** is provided to filter the dashboard based on region.

Available regions include:

- East
- North
- South
- West

Selecting a region allows the user to analyze the corresponding sales data.

---

## 3. Category Slicer

A **Category Slicer** is provided to filter the dashboard by product category.

The dashboard contains categories such as:

- Clothing
- Electronics
- Grocery
- Home Care
- Personal Care

The slicer makes the dashboard interactive and allows users to focus on specific product categories.

---

# 📊 Visualizations

## 4. Total Sales by Category

A column chart is used to compare total sales across different product categories.

This visualization helps identify differences in sales contribution among:

- Clothing
- Electronics
- Grocery
- Home Care
- Personal Care

---

## 5. Monthly Profit

A line chart displays the **monthly profit trend**.

This visualization helps management observe:

- Monthly fluctuations
- High-profit periods
- Low-profit periods
- Overall profit trends

---

## 6. Total Sales Trend

An area chart is used to visualize the overall sales trend.

It provides a graphical representation of sales performance across the selected data.

---

## 7. Total Categories

A pie chart displays the contribution of different product categories.

It provides a visual comparison of category-wise sales contribution.

---

## 8. Region-wise Sales

A pie chart is used to analyze sales distribution across different regions.

The dashboard compares:

- East
- North
- South
- West

This helps identify the contribution of each region to total sales.

---

## 9. City-wise Sales

A horizontal bar chart is used to compare sales across different cities.

This visualization helps management identify cities with higher and lower sales performance.

---

## 10. Top 5 Cities

A column chart displays the **Top 5 Cities based on sales**.

This allows management to quickly identify the cities contributing significantly to overall sales.

---

## 11. Quantity Sold by Category

A pie chart represents the distribution of quantity sold across product categories.

The categories include:

- Clothing
- Electronics
- Grocery
- Home Care
- Personal Care

This visualization helps compare the quantity contribution of each category.
